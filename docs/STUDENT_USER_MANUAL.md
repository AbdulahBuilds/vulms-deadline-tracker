# 🎓 VULMS Smart Deadline Tracker — Complete Student User Manual

Welcome! This manual provides everything you need to know about using the **VULMS Smart Deadline Tracker**.

---

## 🌟 What is the VULMS Smart Deadline Tracker?

The **VULMS Smart Deadline Tracker** is a 100% free, zero-maintenance tool built specifically for Virtual University of Pakistan students. 

- **Automatic Deadline Capture**: It reads your active assignments, quizzes, and GDBs from your VULMS To-Do Calendar when you log into LMS.
- **12:00 PM Email Reminders**: Dispatches an email notification to your inbox at **12:00 PM (noon) PKT on the deadline day**.
- **Runs in Google Cloud**: Your computer does **not** need to stay turned on for reminders to work!
- **100% Safe & Private**: The extension never asks for, reads, or touches your VULMS password.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (In 3 Simple Steps)

### Step 1: Install & Pin the Extension
1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Go to `chrome://extensions/` and enable **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and select the `extension/` folder.
4. Click Chrome's puzzle icon (🧩) in the top right toolbar and click the pin icon (📌) next to **VULMS Smart Deadline Tracker**.

### Step 2: Log In to VULMS as Usual
1. Go to [https://vulms.vu.edu.pk](https://vulms.vu.edu.pk).
2. Log in with your VU credentials and open your **Dashboard / To-Do Calendar**.
3. That’s it! The extension will automatically detect your active deadlines in the background. A green toast notification will appear in the bottom right corner confirming:
   ```
   ✅ Synced X Deadlines to Google Cloud!
   ```

### Step 3: Receive 12:00 PM Noon Email Alerts
- On the day any assignment, quiz, or GDB is due, Google Cloud will automatically send an email reminder at **12:00 PM PKT** directly to your inbox.
- The email includes the course code, activity title, due date, and direct LMS submission link.

---

## ⚙️ Customizing Your Alert Email

By default, alerts are sent to your verified student email address (e.g. `your_id@vu.edu.pk`).

If you prefer receiving reminders in your personal Gmail:
1. Click the **VULMS Tracker** icon in your Chrome toolbar.
2. Click **Alert Email Preference** (at the bottom of the popup).
3. Type your personal Gmail (e.g. `yourname@gmail.com`).
4. Click **Save**.
5. All future alerts will be delivered directly to your personal Gmail!

## 📅 Calendar Sync (Google Calendar & iPhone)

### Google Calendar (Automatic Background Sync & 1-Click Refresh)
1. **100% Background Sync**: Whenever you browse your VULMS dashboard, all active deadlines are automatically synchronized in the background to your dedicated `"🎓 VULMS Deadlines"` Google Calendar in the cloud.
2. **On-Demand Refresh**: If you want to force an immediate refresh anytime without visiting VULMS, open the extension popup and click the **Sync Google Calendar** button.
3. Every calendar entry comes pre-configured with color coding and native reminder notifications on the deadline day.

### iPhone / Apple Calendar / Outlook
- Deadlines can be synchronized to Apple Calendar on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Microsoft Outlook via standard calendar subscription feeds with zero extra apps required.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Does my computer need to stay powered on?
**No.** The reminder engine runs inside Google Cloud. Once deadlines are captured, reminders will arrive on time even if your computer is completely turned off or offline.

### 2. Is my VULMS account password safe?
**Yes, 100%.** The extension has zero access to your password or login credentials. It only extracts publicly displayed course names and due dates on the student calendar page.

### 3. What happens if a deadline is extended (24-hour grace day)?
VULMS frequently grants an automatic 24-hour extension on assignments. When you visit VULMS during the extension period, the tracker detects the revised deadline and automatically reschedules your reminder alert.

### 4. Why is the popup showing 0 deadlines?
- Make sure you visited `https://vulms.vu.edu.pk/Home.aspx` while logged in.
- If you were already on the page when installing the extension, simply press `F5` to refresh the tab.
- Click the **Sync LMS Deadlines** button in the popup to trigger an on-demand rescan.

### 5. Can I use this on multiple computers?
Yes! Install the extension on any computer you use to log in to VULMS. All deadlines sync to the same cloud backend.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about privacy, security, and alerts.

Does my computer need to stay powered on?
No! The reminder system runs 100% in the cloud. Once your deadlines are captured, automated cloud timers dispatch your reminder email at 12:00 PM PKT, even if your laptop is closed, powered off, or offline.
Is my VULMS account password safe?
100% Safe. The extension never asks for, reads, or transmits your LMS password. It only reads the public course codes and due dates displayed on your screen when you are already logged in to VULMS.
Why does the popup show 0 deadlines initially?
If you just installed the extension, simply open your VULMS Home Page or To-Do Calendar. The extension will automatically detect and sync your deadlines within seconds, and a green toast notification will appear in the bottom-right corner confirming the sync.
How do I change which email receives reminders?
Click the extension icon in your Chrome toolbar, click Alert Email Preference at the bottom of the popup, type your personal Gmail address, and click Save. All future deadline emails will go directly to that email.
